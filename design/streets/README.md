# Projects street, golden hour (still, 17 Nov 2026)

A code-drawn still for the street redesign: the home page becomes streets reached by matatu, one per
section (Projects at golden hour, Experience in the morning, Education in Njoro at dawn, the stage in
the CBD at night). This is the test of how much detail code-drawn pixel art can carry, after Cecil
shared a detailed 2D riverside sunset as the bar.

- `projects-street.js` paints the 640x360 scene into one pixel buffer (`paintProjectsStreet(ctx)`).
  Cecil's sprite (`/sprites/cecil-town.png`, frame 4) is drawn on top at x 226, feet at y 302.
- Not wired into the site. Waiting on Cecil's reaction before the matatu, the other streets and the
  move from 426x240 to 640x360.
